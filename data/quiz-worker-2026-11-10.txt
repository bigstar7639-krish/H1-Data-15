Q+A Katha - Cat 🧠

Question: What was the first commerically available computer processor?

Answer: Intel 4004 - Science: Computers. Itha kelvi Cat pathi - Answer Intel 4004. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Cat nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Cat oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Cat pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Cat ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Cat nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Cat ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
Q+A Katha - Dog 🧠

Question: Which of the following is not a real Pokémon?

Answer: Luminid - Entertainment: Video Games. Itha kelvi Dog pathi - Answer Luminid. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Dog pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Dog la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Dog oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
