Q+A Katha - Cat 🧠

Question: What was the first commerically available computer processor?

Answer: Intel 4004 - Science: Computers. Itha kelvi Cat pathi - Answer Intel 4004. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Cat nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Cat oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Cat pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Cat ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Cat nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Cat ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
